## Falsifiable gap statement

For a campus or comparable small institution with limited interval and asset data, can a transparent model estimate *feasible* flexibility ranges, show the assumptions and uncertainty, compare schedules against a no action baseline, and identify the **minimum extra measurements** needed to trust an operational recommendation?

If an existing tool already offers this end to end at similar cost and data burden, the product differentiation must be revised. Pilot interviews should test willingness to use the assessment and ability to supply data.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim grid support from a site simulation without a modeled connection or network constraint and validation.
- Do not infer avoided curtailment, savings, or carbon effects without a traceable baseline and source data.
- Do not optimize against an invented tariff while labeling the result as an Indian bill saving.

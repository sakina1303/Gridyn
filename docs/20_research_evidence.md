## Evidence status for proposed product

- **Verified fact:** the sources above describe an Indian flexibility need, policies, and existing solutions.
- **Research inference:** sparse data and explainability may be an underserved decision problem for small sites.
- **Proposed idea:** a simulation first readiness and decision lab with uncertainty bounds and minimum measurement guidance.
- **Unverified assumption:** campus operators will share interval data and use the recommendations; test through interviews and a shadow pilot.

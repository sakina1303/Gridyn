## Interpretation

The first direction scores highest because it tackles a documented data and visibility barrier while remaining testable without control hardware. The original broad campus optimizer remains a useful **testbed** and becomes more distinctive if it measures confidence, feasibility, and data needs. Transformer and feeder directions are compelling but depend on access to topology and utility measurements that a student team may not have. Generic scheduling, EV charging, and battery sizing face substantial established competition.

These scores should be revised after facility manager and DISCOM interviews, a deeper technical competitor evaluation, and a concrete dataset audit. Evidence behind the scoring is summarized in [the landscape](15_india_landscape.md), [competitor matrix](16_india_competitor_matrix.md), and [gap analysis](17_india_gap_analysis.md).
